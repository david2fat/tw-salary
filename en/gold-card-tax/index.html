How the tax break works

Under Article 9 of the Act for the Recruitment and Employment of Foreign Professionals, a qualifying foreign special professional can exclude 50% of salary above NT$3,000,000 from taxable income. It applies for up to five tax years, starting from the first year the conditions are met.

Who qualifies?

  • You hold an Employment Gold Card, or a work permit as a foreign special professional (特定專業人才), and work for a Taiwan employer.
  • It is your first approved residence in Taiwan for professional work, and you do not have Taiwan household registration. People who were Taiwan residents before may not qualify, so check with the National Taxation Bureau.
  • You stay in Taiwan for 183 days or more in the tax year, so you are taxed as a resident.
  • Your annual salary from that work is over NT$3 million.

Not sure whether you are a resident? The foreigner tax calculator explains the 183-day rule and the 18% non-resident rate.

How to claim it

Apply to the National Taxation Bureau when you file your annual return in May, attaching your work permit or Gold Card, proof of residence days and salary documents. Check the current forms with the bureau before filing.

Other Gold Card tax points

  • Overseas income is generally outside Taiwan's individual income tax, but may count toward the Alternative Minimum Tax if it exceeds NT$1 million and total basic income is above the AMT threshold.
  • If you leave Taiwan before 183 days, salary is taxed at a flat 18% and the exclusion does not apply.

FAQ

Does the Taiwan Gold Card reduce income tax?

Yes, if you are a tax resident earning over NT$3 million from Taiwan work: 50% of the salary above NT$3 million is excluded from taxable income for up to five years.

How much does the Gold Card tax break save?

Using 2026 brackets for a single resident, about NT$300,000 a year on a NT$5 million salary. Savings grow with salary because the excluded part would be taxed at 30% or 40%.

Can I get the Gold Card tax break as a non-resident?

No. You must be in Taiwan for at least 183 days in the tax year. Non-residents pay a flat 18% on Taiwan salary.
