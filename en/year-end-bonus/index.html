What is deducted from a year-end bonus?

  • 5% income tax withholding: applies when a single bonus payment is more than NT$90,500 (2026). It is a prepayment that is settled when you file your return the next May.
  • 2.11% NHI supplementary premium: charged on the part of your year's total bonuses from the same employer that exceeds four times your NHI insured amount. At an insured amount of NT$45,800 the threshold is NT$183,200, so most bonuses are not affected.

Labor Insurance, regular NHI and pension contributions are based on your monthly insured salary, so nothing else is deducted from the bonus.

Will I owe more tax in May?

The bonus is added to your annual salary income. If your net taxable income stays under NT$610,000 (the 5% bracket), the 5% withheld roughly covers it. If part of the bonus falls into the 12% bracket or higher, you pay the difference when you file. If your bonus was under NT$90,500 and nothing was withheld, the tax on it is also due at filing. The calculator shows the expected amount to pay or the refund.

FAQ

Is a year-end bonus required by law?

Article 29 of the Labor Standards Act says a profitable employer should give a bonus or profit share to employees without faults, but sets no amount. If your contract or offer letter guarantees a number of months, the employer must pay it.

Do I get the bonus if I resign before it is paid?

It depends on your employer's rules. A discretionary bonus can require you to be employed on the payment date. A bonus guaranteed as part of your annual pay is usually prorated for the months you worked.

How do I get the withheld 5% back?

You do not apply separately. The withholding statement from your employer is credited against your tax when you file in May, and any overpayment is refunded.
