How take-home pay is calculated in Taiwan

Monthly take-home = salary − Labor Insurance − NHI − voluntary pension. Income tax is usually settled once a year when you file in May, so it is shown separately as a yearly estimate.

  • Labor Insurance: 12.5% rate (11.5% ordinary + 1% employment insurance); employees pay 20%. The insured salary is capped at NT$45,800.
  • National Health Insurance: 5.17% rate; employees pay 30%, plus the same amount for each dependent (up to 3). Insured amount is capped at NT$313,000.
  • Labor Pension: your employer must deposit 6% into your personal account on top of your salary. You may add 0–6% yourself, which is deductible from taxable income. Foreigners qualify if married to a Taiwanese citizen or holding permanent residency.

FAQ

Why is my payslip different from this result?

Common reasons: your employer insures you at a lower grade, tax-free allowances (overtime, meals), monthly tax withholding, or group insurance and welfare fund deductions.

Is the bonus taxed differently?

Bonuses are added to your annual income. If your total bonuses exceed 4× your NHI insured amount, a 2.11% supplementary premium is deducted from the excess.

Should I make voluntary pension contributions?

They reduce taxable income, which helps most if your marginal rate is 12% or higher, but the money is locked until age 60.
