How Taiwan payroll contributions are calculated

A salary is first matched to the official grade table: the insured amount is the lowest grade at or above the salary. Each scheme then applies its own rate, cost split and cap, so on higher salaries the three grades separate.

  • Labor Insurance: 11.5% ordinary accident + 1% employment insurance on the insured salary (capped at NT$45,800). Employee 20%, employer 70%, government 10%.
  • National Health Insurance: 5.17% of the insured amount (capped at NT$313,000). The employee pays 30% for themself and the same again for each dependent, up to three. The employer pays 60% × 1.56 regardless of the employee's actual dependents.
  • Labor Pension: the employer must deposit 6% of monthly wages (capped at NT$150,000) into the worker's individual account. This is not deducted from pay. Workers may add up to 6% themselves, which is tax-deductible.
  • Occupational Accident Insurance: paid entirely by the employer at an industry rate of 0.05%–0.89% plus 0.07% for commuting accidents, on wages up to NT$72,800.

FAQ

Do foreigners pay Labor Insurance and NHI?

Yes. Foreign employees with an ARC are enrolled in Labor, Occupational Accident and Health Insurance from their first day. Employment Insurance and the Labor Pension generally apply only to foreign spouses of Taiwan citizens and permanent residents (APRC), so untick those options otherwise.

Are bonuses and overtime included in the insured salary?

Regular monthly allowances count; irregular overtime and year-end bonuses usually do not. Bonuses above 4× the NHI insured amount in a year carry a 2.11% supplementary premium.

My employer insures me at a lower grade. Is that allowed?

No. Under-reporting is illegal and lowers your future Labor Insurance benefits, unemployment pay and pension. You can check your insured salary in the Bureau of Labor Insurance e-service or the NHI Express app.

What changed in 2026?

The minimum wage rose to NT$29,500, so the lowest full-time grade moved up from NT$28,590. Rates, caps and the NHI average dependents figure (0.56) are unchanged from 2025.
