Rest day vs regular day off

Of the two days off each week, one is a regular day off (例假日) and one is a rest day (休息日). Your employer may ask you to work on the rest day if they pay the rates above, and those hours count toward the monthly cap. Work on a regular day off is only allowed for natural disasters or emergencies, with an extra day's wage and a compensatory day off.

Overtime limits

Normal hours plus overtime may not exceed 12 hours a day, and overtime may not exceed 46 hours a month. With union or labor-management meeting consent the cap can be 54 hours a month and 138 hours per 3 months.

Comp time instead of pay

You can choose time off instead of overtime pay, one hour for each hour worked, but your employer cannot force it. If the comp time is not used by the agreed deadline or when you leave, it must be paid out.

FAQ

How is hourly pay calculated for overtime in Taiwan?

For a monthly salary, divide by 240 (30 days × 8 hours). A NT$40,000 salary gives an hourly base of NT$166.67.

What is the weekday overtime rate in Taiwan?

The first 2 overtime hours are paid at 1⅓ times the hourly wage, and hours 3 and 4 at 1⅔ times.

How much do you get for working on a national holiday in Taiwan?

Within 8 hours you receive one extra day's wage, so the day is paid double. Hours 9–10 are paid at 1⅓ and hours 11–12 at 1⅔.

Is overtime pay taxed in Taiwan?

Overtime pay within the 46-hour monthly cap, including rest-day work, is tax-free. Pay for hours above the cap is taxed as salary.
