How Taiwan income tax is calculated

Tax = net taxable income × rate − progressive difference. Net taxable income = total income − exemptions − standard or itemized deduction − special deductions. This applies to tax residents; non-residents are taxed at a flat 18% (see the foreigner tax calculator).

Married couples

Spouses must file one return, but either spouse's salary can be taxed separately. The calculator compares both methods and uses the lower one.

FAQ

How much income is tax-free in Taiwan?

A single resident with only salary income pays no tax on up to NT$464,000 a year for 2026 (exemption + standard deduction + salary deduction).

What are the Taiwan income tax brackets for 2026?

5% up to NT$610,000 of net taxable income, 12% up to NT$1,380,000, 20% up to NT$2,770,000, 30% up to NT$5,190,000 and 40% above.

When do I file my 2026 Taiwan tax return?

Between May 1 and May 31, 2027. Foreigners leaving Taiwan for good must file before departure.
