About Taiwan Salary Tools

Free Taiwan payroll and labor-law calculators. No sign-up, and your numbers never leave your browser.

What this site does

Taiwan Salary Tools turns Taiwan's Labor Standards Act, Labor Insurance, National Health Insurance and income tax rules into simple calculators, so employees, graduates and foreigners working in Taiwan can see what they will actually be paid.

Sources

  • Bureau of Labor Insurance, Ministry of Labor: insured salary grades and Labor Pension contribution grades
  • National Health Insurance Administration: NHI premium grades, rates and supplementary premium rules
  • Ministry of Finance: income tax exemptions, deductions, brackets and withholding rates
  • Laws and Regulations Database of the ROC: Labor Standards Act, Labor Pension Act, Employment Insurance Act

How often it is updated

Every January when the new year's grades and minimum wage take effect, and within a week of any new official rate or law change. Each page shows its last-updated month at the bottom.
