Taiwan Minimum Wage 2027

From January 1, 2027 the monthly minimum wage rises from NT$29,500 to NT$30,900 and the hourly minimum wage from NT$196 to NT$205.

Does it apply to foreigners?

Yes for foreign employees covered by the Labor Standards Act, including white-collar foreign professionals. Live-in caregivers and domestic helpers are not covered by the Labor Standards Act and have a separate minimum set by the Ministry of Labor.

What counts toward the minimum wage?

Pay for normal working hours, including regular allowances such as meal or attendance allowances. Overtime pay and holiday work premiums do not count, and neither do one-off bonuses.

FAQ

What is the minimum wage in Taiwan in 2027?

From January 1, 2027 it is NT$30,900 a month and NT$205 an hour, a 4.745% rise on the monthly rate.

What was the minimum wage in Taiwan in 2026?

NT$29,500 a month and NT$196 an hour.

How much is take-home pay on Taiwan's minimum wage?

On the 2026 minimum wage of NT$29,500, take-home pay is about NT$28,304 after Labor Insurance and NHI.
