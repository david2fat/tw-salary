Notice period

  • 3 months to under 1 year of service: 10 days
  • 1 to under 3 years: 20 days
  • 3 years or more: 30 days

If you are let go without this notice, the employer owes wages for the notice period. During the notice period you may take up to two days a week of paid leave to look for a new job.

When are you owed severance?

When the employer terminates you under Article 11 (closure, losses, downsizing, change of business, or inability to do the job), or when you quit under Article 14 because the employer broke the law (unpaid wages, no insurance, illegal working conditions). You are not owed severance if you resign voluntarily, if a fixed-term contract ends normally, or if you are dismissed for serious misconduct under Article 12.

FAQ

Is severance pay taxed?

Severance is treated as retirement income, which has a tax-free allowance per year of service. Typical severance amounts fall within it.

When must it be paid?

Within 30 days after the contract ends. If it is late, you can file a complaint or request mediation with the local labor bureau.

Do I get severance during probation?

Yes. Taiwan law has no probation exception. If you are laid off under Article 11 after one month, you are owed a prorated amount.
